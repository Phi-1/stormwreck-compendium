
## Introduction

Hi Oz!

Allow me to introduce yourself to you. You're a cleric for the order of Torm, Right Hand of Justice. You specialize in finding traces of magic, whether to uncover unholy rituals or long-lost divine relics. 
Your latest assignment has the order sending you to the Astral plane, where traces of an unknown magic have been appearing. 
The Astral plane is another dimension of reality, which fills the void in between the individual realms of the many gods. It is a timeless place, literally. Time does not exist. It will still pass in the outside world, but while you're in it, you do not age, no matter decays, and it's impossible to keep track of time. By the time you exit this place, months could have passed, millennia even, but it would all feel the same to you. 
At some point during your exploration of this realm, you began seeing and feeling pulses of powerful magic originating from somewhere in the distance, accompanied by a strange light in the sky. It faded some time later - who knows how long that was - but you still managed to find the rough location from where it came. 
The area around you there was absolutely littered with planar scars leading to the Elemental Plane of Fire, as well as traces of powerful magic.
This is where you'll start off on your journey through Stormwreck Isle and beyond, and I look forward to seeing you there!

Also, you asked for cats. Koen *specifically* told me that you asked him to tell me that you really like cats, so I gave you a cat. In spirit form. Because you also asked for teleports. And this spirit cat can help you cast teleportation spells. 
I think that's pretty neat. 
I'll leave the details of how you found and bonded this cat up to you. All you need to do to summon it is flip the coin that's in your pocket.

In addition to this, you have a magical ring that you can use to regain one of your spell slots. Spell slots are required to cast spells. A spell of level one requires you to use a spell slot of level 1 or higher. The number of spell slots you have is determined by your level as a cleric, and you normally only regain them when you sleep. This ring can refuel one spell slot for you once a day.

### Feats

Your Human species grants you the Observant passive ability, which makes you more generally observant (No way!). This is mostly something I need to pay attention to, to tell you about things you notice, like enemies trying to sneak up on your party, but it also grants you the ability to read people's lips.

### Spells

Now onto your spells. Your spells are probably the most complex part of playing as a cleric, so we'll take it one step at a time.
Like I mentioned before, spells have a level. This level ranges from zero to nine, a level nine spell being the most powerful. Casting a spell requires you to spend a spell slot of the same level as the spell, or of a higher level. Spells often increase in power when cast with a higher level spell slot.
As a level three cleric, you have four level one spell slots available, and two level two slots. You will get more as you level up. You regain your used spell slots every time you sleep.

Now, you may have noticed that even though I said spells range from level zero to nine, you have no level zero spell slots. That's because level zero spells are special. More often than level zero spells, they're called cantrips, and they're available to you at all times, for free. You only know a limited number of them, and they're generally not as powerful as higher level spells, but you can cast them at any time you want, as often as you want. 
You know a cantrip called Thaumaturgy, that can create a small illusion, Sacred Flame, which you've used to blast some cultists before, and Spare the Dying, which can help you and your allies stay alive.
You'll also learn more cantrips as you level up.

Clerics have a huge list of spells available to them to choose from. Healing spells, revival spells, teleportation spells, shielding spells, spells that burn undead, spells that find demons, there are just a ton of them.
However, you can't just use any spell from that list at any time. For you to be able to cast a spell, you need to have that spell prepared. Every time you wake up from a long rest, you can choose a certain number of spells from the cleric spell list to prepare for that day.
This has two limitations: you can only prepare spells of a level for which you have spell slots, and the number of spells you can prepare is determined by your cleric level and your Wisdom. 
Right now, you can prepare six spells a day, and they can be level one or two spells.
I've already prepared six spells for you on your character sheet to start off with, but feel free to choose different ones. The entire spell list can be found here: https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/spells:cleric

Every spell also has something called components. These come in three forms: Verbal, Somatic, and Material, abbreviated to V, S, and M. 
Verbal means the spell requires you to say something when you cast it. This is mostly for flavour, but if you're ever in a situation where you can't speak or make sound, spells with this component are impossible to cast.
Somatic means you have to make a movement or sign as part of the spell, which requires you to have a hand free. You'll often have to stow away your mace when casting a spell.
The Material component of a spell dictates ingredients the spell requires, like sage leaves, or bone fragments. These are not required if you're using your holy symbol to cast the spell, except when the spell states that whatever materials it requires, it also consumes. In that case you'll have to have the necessary materials on hand when casting the spell. For example, the revivify spell requires diamonds that are worth at least 300 gold pieces, which it consumes. You'd have to buy these diamonds somewhere beforehand so you're ready to revive someone when it comes down to it.

One more thing, then we're done with spells. As a cleric, your divine powers are granted to you by the deity you follow. Each deity has a certain specialty when it comes to spells. In your case, your cat belongs to the Twilight Domain.
Through this domain, you get access to a small list of spells that are normally unavailable to clerics. You don't need to prepare these spells. They basically always count as being prepared.
These spells are also on your character sheet.

### Abilities

As a cleric, you get access to several unique abilities. For now you have four.

Let's start with the simplest one, Eyes of Night.
Through the power of magic and cats, but mostly the Twilight Domain, you can see in the dark. Really well. Most people that can see in the dark can do so with a range of 60 feet. You have darkvision up to 300 feet. You can also share this darkvision with your allies for one hour, once per short or long rest.

Vigilant Blessing is an ability that allows you to give someone you touch advantage on the next initiative roll they make. We'll explain what initiative rolls are and what advantage is when we're playing. Simply put, it gives one creature a boost at the start of combat, as long as you use this ability before combat begins. 

Then on to Channel Divinity. Once per a long rest, you can channel divine energy from your deity to create a certain effect. You can choose from two effects right now, but that list will grow as you level up.
- Channel Divinity number one, Turn Undead:
You make undead creatures run for their lives! AAAAAH!
- Channel Divinity number two, Twilight Sanctuary:
A sphere of twilight emanates from you, safeguarding you and your allies. The sphere moves with you, has a 30-foot radius, and lasts for one minute. Whenever you or an ally ends its turn in the sphere, you can choose to give them 1d6 + your cleric level temporary hit points, or cure them of one effect that causes them to be charmed or frightened.
Channel Divinity can be used more times in between rests when you reach higher levels.

Final one!
You can use up one use of your Channel Divinity to regain one expended spell slot. The level of that spell slot can't be higher than half your proficiency bonus. For now that's a level one spell slot.

##### Feats
Channel Divinity: 
Once per short or long rest, channel Turn Undead or Twilight Sanctuary.

Turn Undead: 
As an action, you present your holy symbol and speak a prayer censuring the undead. Each undead that can see or hear you within 30 feet of you must make a Wisdom saving throw. If the creature fails its saving throw, it is turned for 1 minute or until it takes any damage.
A turned creature must spend its turns trying to move as far away from you as it can, and it can't willingly move to a space within 30 feet of you. It also can't take reactions. For its action, it can use only the Dash action or try to escape from an effect that prevents it from moving. If there's nowhere to move, the creature can use the Dodge action.

Twilight Sanctuary:
As an action, you present your holy symbol, and a sphere of twilight emanates from you. The sphere is centered on you, has a 30-foot radius, and is filled with dim light. The sphere moves with you, and it lasts for 1 minute or until you are incapacitated or die. Whenever a creature (including you) ends its turn in the sphere, you can grant that creature one of these benefits:
- You grant it temporary hit points equal to 1d6 plus your cleric level.
- You end one effect on it causing it to be charmed or frightened.

Harness Divine Power:
You can expend a use of your Channel Divinity to fuel your spells. As a bonus action, you touch your holy symbol, utter a prayer, and regain one expended spell slot, the level of which can be no higher than half your proficiency bonus (rounded up). The number of times you can use this feature is based on the level you've reached in this class: 2nd level, once; 6th level, twice; and 18th level, thrice. You regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

Eyes of Night:
You can see through the deepest gloom. You have darkvision out to a range of 300 feet. In that radius, you can see in dim light as if it were bright light and in darkness as if it were dim light.
As an action, you can magically share the darkvision of this feature with willing creatures you can see within 10 feet of you, up to a number of creatures equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum of one creature). The shared darkvision lasts for 1 hour. Once you share it, you can't do so again until you finish a long rest, unless you expend a spell slot of any level to share it again.

Vigilant Blessing:
The night has taught you to be vigilant. As an action, you give one creature you touch (including possibly yourself) advantage on the next initiative roll the creature makes. This benefit ends immediately after the roll or if you use this feature again.


## On your turn

You have six seconds to:
- Move up to 30 feet
- Use an action
- Use a bonus action
- Talk
- Interact with your environment

## Actions

- Attack with your mace or your crossbow. Attacking with your crossbow requires two free hands
- Cast a spell that has a casting time of one action
- Use a feature that requires an action
- Shove a creature
- Grapple a creature
- Disengage: move without provoking opportunity attacks
- Dodge: enemies have disadvantage on attack rolls against you until your next turn
- Dash: double your movement speed for this turn
- Help: give an ally advantage on their next ability check or attack roll
- Hide: when unseen by another creature, attempt to obscure your presence from them (you have disadvantage on stealth checks while you're wearing heavy armor)
- Hold: prepare an action for a specific scenario, like an enemy walking into your attack range. The prepared action gains precedence over other creature's actions
- Perform a skill check
- Perform a time-consuming interaction with an object, like throwing a lever, or wrenching open a door
- Improvise: go nuts, I'll tell you if doing something would take up an action

## Spells

Your cantrips are simple spells that are always available and cost nothing to cast. 

At the end of every long rest, you can choose six (for now) spells to prepare from the entire Cleric spell list. I've chosen six for you to start off with.
On top of these, you have four spells that you've always got prepared, and that don't count towards the maximum number of spells you can have prepared. I've marked these as (Domain) spells here.

### Cantrips:

##### Light:
**Casting Time:** 1 action  
**Range:** Touch  
**Components:** V, M (a firefly or phosphorescent moss)  
**Duration:** 1 hour

You touch one object that is no larger than 10 feet in any dimension. Until the spell ends, the object sheds bright light in a 20-foot radius and dim light for an additional 20 feet. The light can be colored as you like. Completely covering the object with something opaque blocks the light. The spell ends if you cast it again or dismiss it as an action.

If you target an object held or worn by a hostile creature, that creature must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw to avoid the spell.

##### Resistance:
**Casting Time:** 1 action  
**Range:** Touch  
**Components:** V, S, M (a miniature cloak)  
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You touch one willing creature. Once before the spell ends, the target can roll a d4 and add the number rolled to one saving throw of its choice. It can roll the die before or after the saving throw. The spell then ends.

##### Sacred Flame:
**Casting Time:** 1 action  
**Range:** 60 feet  
**Components:** V, S  
**Duration:** Instantaneous

Flame-like radiance descends on a creature that you can see within range. The target must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or take 1d8 radiant damage. The target gains no benefit from cover for this saving throw.

**_At Higher Levels._** The spell’s damage increases by 1d8 when you reach 5th level (2d8), 11th level (3d8), and 17th level (4d8).

### Prepared Spells:

##### Faerie Fire (Domain):
_1st-level evocation_
**Casting Time:** 1 action  
**Range:** 60 feet  
**Components:** V  
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

Each object in a 20-foot cube within range is outlined in blue, green, or violet light (your choice).

Any creature in the area when the spell is cast is also outlined in light if it fails a Dexterity saving throw. For the duration, objects and affected creatures shed dim light in a 10-foot radius.

Any attack roll against an affected creature or object has advantage if the attacker can see it, and the affected creature or object can’t benefit from being invisible.

##### Sleep (Domain):
_1st-level enchantment_
**Casting Time:** 1 action  
**Range:** 90 feet  
**Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of fine sand, rose petals, or a cricket)  
**Duration:** 1 minute

This spell sends creatures into a magical slumber. Roll 5d8, the total is how many hit points of creatures this spell can affect. Creatures within 20 feet of a point you choose within range are affected in ascending order of their current hit points (ignoring unconscious creatures).

Starting with the creature that has the lowest current hit points, each creature affected by this spell falls unconscious until the spell ends, the sleeper takes damage, or someone uses an action to shake or slap the sleeper awake. Subtract each creature’s hit points from the total before moving on to the creature with the next lowest hit points. A creature’s hit points must be equal to or less than the remaining total for that creature to be affected. Undead and creatures immune to being charmed aren’t affected by this spell.

**_At Higher Levels._** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, roll an additional 2d8 for each slot level above 1st.

##### Moonbeam (Domain):
_2nd-level evocation_
**Casting Time:** 1 action  
**Range:** 120 feet  
**Components:** V, S, M (several seeds of any moonseed plant and a piece of opalescent feldspar)  
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

A silvery beam of pale light shines down in a 5-foot radius, 40-foot-high cylinder centered on a point within range. Until the spell ends, dim light fills the cylinder.

When a creature enters the spell’s area for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there, it is engulfed in ghostly flames that cause searing pain, and it must make a Constitution saving throw. It takes 2d10 radiant damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

A shapechanger makes its saving throw with disadvantage. If it fails, it also instantly reverts to its original form and can’t assume a different form until it leaves the spell’s light.

On each of your turns after you cast this spell, you can use an action to move the beam up to 60 feet in any direction.

**_At Higher Levels._** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d10 for each slot level above 2nd.

**Range:** 30 feet

##### Bane:
_1st-level enchantment_
**Casting Time:** 1 action  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Components:** V, S, M (a drop of blood)  
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

Up to three creatures of your choice that you can see within range must make Charisma saving throws. Whenever a target that fails this saving throw makes an attack roll or a saving throw before the spell ends, the target must roll a d4 and subtract the number rolled from the attack roll or saving throw.

**_At Higher Levels._** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, you can target one additional creature for each slot level above 1st.

##### Command:
_1st-level enchantment_
**Casting Time:** 1 action  
**Range:** 60 feet  
**Components:** V  
**Duration:** 1 round

You speak a one-word command to a creature you can see within range. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or follow the command on its next turn. The spell has no effect if the target is undead, if it doesn’t understand your language, or if your command is directly harmful to it. Some typical commands and their effects follow. You might issue a command other than one described here. If you do so, the DM determines how the target behaves. If the target can’t follow your command, the spell ends.

**_Approach._** The target moves toward you by the shortest and most direct route, ending its turn if it moves within 5 feet of you.

**_Drop._** The target drops whatever it is holding and then ends its turn.

**_Flee._** The target spends its turn moving away from you by the fastest available means.

**_Grovel._** The target falls prone and then ends its turn.

**_Halt._** The target doesn’t move and takes no actions. A flying creature stays aloft, provided that it is able to do so. If it must move to stay aloft, it flies the minimum distance needed to remain in the air.

**_At Higher Levels._** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, you can affect one additional creature for each slot level above 1st. The creatures must be within 30 feet of each other when you target them.

##### Detect Evil and Good:
_1st-level divination_
**Casting Time:** 1 action  
**Range:** Self  
**Components:** V, S  
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

For the duration, you know if there is an aberration, celestial, elemental, fey, fiend, or undead within 30 feet of you, as well as where the creature is located. Similarly, you know if there is a place or object within 30 feet of you that has been magically consecrated or desecrated.

The spell can penetrate most barriers, but it is blocked by 1 foot of stone, 1 inch of common metal, a thin sheet of lead, or 3 feet of wood or dirt.

##### Lesser Restoration:
_2nd-level abjuration_
**Casting Time:** 1 action  
**Range:** Touch  
**Components:** V, S  
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You touch a creature and can end either one disease or one condition afflicting it. The condition can be blinded, deafened, paralyzed, or poisoned.
