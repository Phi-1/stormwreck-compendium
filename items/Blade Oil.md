# Blade Oil

Take 1 minute to apply to any non-magical weapon or piece of ammunition, or magical ones that are only +1,  +2 or +3. 
Provides a temporary magical effect.

## Oil of Faerie Fire
*uncommon blade oil, lasts for 1 hour*
On hitting a creature with an attack, explode into faerie fire, forcing every creature except the wielder within 5 feet of the target to make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw or be subject to the effects of the faerie fire spell until the start of the wielder's next turn.

## Oil of Empowering
*uncommon blade oil, lasts for 4 hours*
Adds a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this weapon, and makes its damage magical if it wasn't already.

## Oil of Switching
*rare blade oil, lasts for 1 hour*
A creature hit by an attack must make a DC 15 Charisma saving throw or magically swap places with the wielder. 
This effect does not work if the target does not fit in the wielder's space or vice versa.
