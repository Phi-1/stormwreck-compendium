# Constellation of the Centaur

Once a day, you may enter a wild rage, granting you resistance to nonmagical bludgeoning, slashing, and piercing damage, becoming unable to cast or concentrate on spells, gaining 10 feet of movement, and making your melee attacks entangle your target in vines (escape DC 14). The rage lasts until you have not damaged a creature with a melee attack or been hit by an attack for a round.
